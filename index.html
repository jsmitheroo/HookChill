how to play

hold (click / tap / space) to grab the nearest lantern.
release to let go and fly — every release gives a little hop, so spam to climb.
spin in the air for flips. chain grabs quickly for a combo, and grab a lantern just above the fog for a clutch bonus.
bubbles slow time · portals teleport you · updrafts lift you · red lanterns burn out after one use · crumbly platforms bounce you once.
bounce pads launch you · rings boost you · spikes & the fog end the tape.
every run earns ✦ points (records ◉ add bonus) — spend them on hats, colours & trails.
